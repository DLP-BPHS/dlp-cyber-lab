
# Day 1: The 3:17 Mystery

**Topic:** Digital Forensics & Evidence-Based Reasoning  
**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Audience:** Middle School

## Overview
Students investigate a fictional school computer incident using 30 facts about three suspects. Working in groups, they analyze evidence, develop theories, and defend their conclusions.

## Learning Objectives
- Analyze and interpret evidence.
- Distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Build arguments supported by evidence.
- Recognize uncertainty in investigations.

## Activity
1. Introduce the 3:17 PM computer incident.
2. Give groups the three suspect profiles.
3. Allow 10 minutes for investigation and discussion.
4. Have each group present a 60-second argument.
5. If time allows, give each group 5 minutes to create an argument for a suspect they did not choose.

**Note:** There is no predetermined correct answer. Students must defend their own conclusions.
